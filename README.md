# ZapFlex – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

ZapFlex FZC is a Dubai-based fitness and wellness company offering EMS training, personal training, private Pilates, health management consultations, and professional fitness education. The business serves individuals seeking personalised fitness solutions, time-efficient workouts, and professional training guidance.

The redesigned homepage focuses on positioning ZapFlex as a personalised fitness and wellness provider, making its services easier to explore, and highlighting trainer expertise to encourage visitors to enquire and book through WhatsApp or direct contact.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Positioning
- Replaced broad introductory messaging with a more specific presentation of ZapFlex's personalised fitness and EMS training services.
- Improved the communication of its fitness approach, key offerings, and customer benefits.
- Created a clearer first impression to help visitors understand what distinguishes ZapFlex and which services may suit their goals.

### 2. Improved Service Discovery and Customer Journey
- Organised EMS training, personal training, Pilates, health consultations, and fitness education into clearer service sections.
- Improved service descriptions to help visitors understand the available training options.
- Introduced clearer pathways between service discovery and individual enquiries, making it easier to find relevant solutions and contact the business.

### 3. Stronger Professional Credibility and Trainer Visibility
- Improved the presentation of trainer profiles, qualifications, experience, and specialisations.
- Gave greater visibility to the professional expertise behind ZapFlex's training services.
- Strengthened the credibility section to help potential customers make more informed decisions before enquiring.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Applied the approved charcoal (#2B2C32), blue (#7BAAED), black, and white colour palette to create a modern fitness-focused appearance.
- **Layout:** Improved section hierarchy, spacing, alignment, and organisation of services and trainer information.
- **Typography:** Established clearer distinctions between headlines, service titles, descriptions, and supporting content.
- **User Experience:** Simplified service discovery and improved access to WhatsApp and direct contact options.
- **Consistency:** Standardised service presentation, trainer profiles, buttons, and calls to action across the homepage.
- **Overall Appearance:** Created a more cohesive and professional homepage that communicates ZapFlex's combination of EMS technology, personalised coaching, Pilates, and fitness education.
